The Incident at Outpost 7

Three days ago, Deep Space Platform Aethelgard-IV went dark. Life support collapsed, containment vault protocols were severed, and the emergency beacon began broadcasting on loop.

DOSSIER FILE // 01

The Meltdown

The subdeck reactor is undergoing thermal runaway. The automated coolants were redirected by a catastrophic system fault. You must descend into the irradiated reactor subdeck and manually operate thermal release valves before the containment hull breaches.

DOSSIER FILE // 02

The Unseen Predator

Whatever was housed in the sub-orbital cryogenic lab is now roaming freely. It hunts via spatial sound reflection and footstep acoustics. It maneuvers through ceiling ventilation ducts and traps unsuspecting survivors in darkened airlocks.

DOSSIER FILE // 03

The Lone Engineer

You are not a combat specialist. You have no firearms, body armor, or plasma shields. Your only tools are diagnostic tablets, magnetic maintenance keys, chemical gas flushes, and the station's labyrinthine secondary power grid.

Subsystem Engineering
